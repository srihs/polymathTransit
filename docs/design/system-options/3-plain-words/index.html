PolymathTransit Design direction 3 of 5: Plain Words

Plain Words

1. About this direction

The interface reads like a clear note from the transport office. One column, large type, no decoration: every screen answers "what do I do now?" in words first.

Its signature is a sentence. As you fill in the form, PolymathTransit writes your request back to you in one plain sentence: the same kind of sentence the coordinator reads on the dashboard and the driver reads as the next stop.

Principles

  1. Words first. If a sentence can do it, use a sentence. Counts are written as sentences, statuses are words, explanations are paragraphs.
  2. One column, one thing at a time, in the order people think. Who you are, where and when, then anything special.
  3. Big enough to read without glasses, in sunlight. 19 px body text, 21 px questions, 16 px at the smallest, black on white.
  4. Nothing on the page without a job. Only four things get a box: the sentence, the error summary, the selected booking and the status badges.

2. Logo

The full logo carries its own white ground, so it sits on the white page as it is. The dashed line shows the clear space: a quarter of the crest's height on every side. The full logo is never shown narrower than 240 px.

The crest on light, dark and purple

Sizes and misuse

Smallest sizes: the crest 24 px tall (the letterhead uses 40 px); the full logo 240 px wide. Favicon: crest.png.

  • Don't stretch, squash, rotate or recolour the crest, for example to match a status colour.
  • Don't set the words "Polymath College" in Atkinson Hyperlegible as if they were the logo. The product name, PolymathTransit, is set in the type; the college's name appears only in the artwork.

3. Colour

Black words on white, the crest purple for actions, links, focus and the sentence's rule. Every other colour has one job: a status or a signal, always with a word and an icon beside it. Ratios are calculated with the WCAG 2.2 formula; text needs 4.5:1 (AA) or 7:1 (AAA), lines and controls 3:1.

4. Type

Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for everything, because its letters and figures cannot be mistaken for one another (I, l and 1; O and 0), which matters for phone numbers and references. Atkinson Hyperlegible Mono for references and the driver's phone numbers. Noto Sans Sinhala for Sinhala. Body text never goes below 16 px.

8. Signature element: the plain sentence

As the form is answered, a box headed "Check your request" writes the request back as one sentence. Answers appear in bold; anything still missing shows as a gap in [brackets], and each gap is a link to its question. The coordinator's rows and panel lead with the same kind of sentence, and the driver's next stop is one. Its job: one last check before sending, in the words a person would use, which is faster than re-reading twelve fields (BO-04) and catches wrong requests before a coordinator has to.

Part answered

Check your request

A van for 4 people from Branch B to the Sports Centre on [date], picking up at [pickup time], [return trip?].

Words in [brackets] are still missing. Choose one to answer it.

Complete

Check your request

A van for 4 people from Branch B to the Sports Centre on Tuesday 13/10/2026, picking up at 8:45 am (± 10 min), coming back at 10:45 am.

If this is right, send your request.

Complete, in Sinhala (draft, D-09)

9. Motion

None. Plain Words does not animate: nothing slides, fades or spins. Things that change simply change, and the words say so.

What happens instead of motion
ChangeWhat the person seesWith reduced motion
Send request with errorsFocus moves to the error summary at once; no scrolling animation.The same
Send request without errorsThe confirmation replaces the form and focus moves to its heading at once.The same
An answer is givenThe sentence updates at once; screen readers hear it after a 1.5-second pause.The same
Approve, Done, No-showThe status word changes, with a message and Undo for 10 seconds.The same

A prefers-reduced-motion: reduce rule still switches off any animation or transition a browser or a later change might add.
